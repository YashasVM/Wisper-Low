## Intent

Promo film for the Wisperlow Android voice keyboard. Make it engaging, visually
stunning, "way too impressive". Viewers must understand it is local
(on-device) speech-to-text inside a beautiful keyboard UI. Buttery-smooth motion.

## Customizations

- Hero 3D shot (user's idea): keys fall slowly, each landing sends a bloom/repel
  shockwave, keys orbit the camera, then snap into the phone to form the keyboard.
- Everything in DARK MODE — palette from android KeyboardTheme.kt `Dark`.
- Background music: cinematic electronic, ~100 BPM, drop on keyboard formation.
- Voiceover: warm, calm, confident; music ducked under it. SFX: key thocks, whooshes, glass shimmer.
- CTA: Play Store + website.
- 9:16 recut offered later.

## Assets

- assets/fonts/ — Instrument Serif, Inter, JetBrains Mono (the app's own type system).
