(01) The idea

You think faster than you type. Wisperlow lets you just say it — a global shortcut, a local Whisper model on your GPU, and the text lands in Slack, your editor or your email. Your audio never leaves the machine, because there's no server to send it to.

(03) How it works

Four steps.
Zero servers.

01
Press
Global shortcut

Push-to-talk or toggle, from any app. Or trigger it from scripts with --toggle-transcription.

02
Listen
Silero VAD

The mic is captured and resampled, and voice activity detection drops the silence before inference.

03
Transcribe
Whisper · Parakeet

The model runs locally, accelerated by Metal on macOS and Vulkan on Windows and Linux.

04
Paste
Active window

Text is inserted where your cursor is and saved to your local history.
