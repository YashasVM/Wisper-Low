( how it works )

Three steps. No sign-up.

01

Install and pick a model

Choose the multilingual model (25 languages) or the small English one for older phones. It downloads once.

02

Tap the mic and talk

The keyboard turns violet, your music pauses, and words appear as you speak. Pause and it stops on its own.

03

It lands in the field

Punctuated, cleaned up and spelled your way, in whatever app you were already using.

Voice typing shouldn't mean shipping your words to someone else's computer.
