Why Wisperlow

Dictation that respects
your privacy and your time.

Everything runs on your machine with GPU acceleration, so it's fast, private and works offline.

🔒

Private by architecture

Audio is processed by models running on your own CPU or GPU. There's no transcription server to trust, because there isn't one.

⚡

GPU accelerated

Metal on macOS, Vulkan on Windows and Linux.

⌨️

Works in every app

Text is pasted straight into whatever window has focus.

🎙️

Push-to-talk or toggle

Configurable global shortcuts, with voice activity detection to trim silence.

🌍

Dozens of languages

Multilingual models plus built-in translation to English.

📱

Desktop and Android

Native apps for macOS, Windows and Linux, plus an Android keyboard bubble that dictates into any text field.

🕘

History built in

Every transcription is saved locally so you can find and reuse it.
