Free, no accountRelease notes

( why I built it )

Voice typing shouldn't need to ship your words somewhere.

Every phone keyboard has a mic button, and most of them send what you say to a server. I didn't want my messages, notes and half-finished thoughts going through someone else's computer just so I could avoid typing.

Wisperlow does the whole thing on the phone: the speech model, voice detection, cleanup, your personal dictionary and history. Once the model is downloaded you can switch on airplane mode and it keeps working. No account, no ads, no cloud detour.

I also didn't want to replace my keyboard. So instead of a new keyboard, it's a small bubble that sits next to the one you already have: Gboard, Samsung, SwiftKey, anything. Tap it, talk, pause, and the text lands in whatever field you're in.

It runs NVIDIA's Parakeet models, which are fast enough to feel instant on a normal phone. English plus 24 European languages by default, with a smaller English-only model for older devices.

It's free and open source on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does with your voice: nothing.
